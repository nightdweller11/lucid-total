> Chronological record of the owner's instructions and of decisions taken (by the owner, or by the lead pending the owner's confirmation). Entries are `T<n>` (to keep them distinct from Lucid Chat's `L<n>`). Material open questions are listed at the end and, once Lucid Total runs, become decision items in the owner's inbox. ## 2026-10-27 / 2026-10-28 · Founding brief (owner instruction)

- **T1 (owner, 2026-10-27).** "Create a new task dashboard tool for Lucid and move our development operation to run through it." Tasks carry everything needed to understand and build them (context, technical data, infrastructure), what is expected (definition of done, mocks, criteria) and the actual result, compared easily; a comment area for feedback before, during and after, which can update the task; dependencies both ways; human tasks (accounts, cards, testing, feedback, decisions); all tasks tracked and updated when done; card view collapsible to line view; filtering, time tracking, issues (support, errors, bugs, system); a unified company tracking system. → `docs/01` §A–§B, `docs/02` §4–§5.
- **T2 (owner).** Embedded AI with model choice including Fable; "tell it what I want and it does it": follow up, reminders, emails to users about tasks that concern them, notifications through the system. Start without SSO; Google SSO is the first task in the system. → `docs/02` §7–§8, `docs/11` LT-0001.
- **T3 (owner).** "Translate (and in the process evaluate) all the work that has been done." The checklist is replaced; a live report of what any agent is working on, "I don't want over reporting, but I do want to track where things stand." → `docs/10`, `docs/02` §5.6, `docs/08` §5.
- **T4 (owner).** A Claude agentic framework with a full modern chat; access to task data, codebase, documents, everything; an in-memory RAG learning from appfront's methods (not its code). Final and actual work is always done by the Claude Code coordinator (Fable, or Opus when context is short); answers, assistance and task updates by the embedded AI. **Working principle everywhere:** anything a user can do in the interface, the AI can do on their behalf. Secure storage and transport for API keys and the like through the chat and the app in dedicated windows. Use Lucid Chat's theming; must work well on a PC and on mobile; testing included and properly executed. → `docs/00` principles 2, 9, 11; `docs/06`; `docs/07` §5; `docs/09`; `docs/research/`.
- **T5 (owner).** The name is **Lucid Total**; a separate project in a separate repository (`nightdweller11/lucid-total`), in its own folder on the machine, not inside the Lucid Chat area. "In the end we'll even sell Lucid Total." It will also be wired into the Lucid Chat app through its API: messages like "you need to call the investor to set a meeting" and "these employees aren't doing well". → `docs/01` §J, §E (UC-E4 phrased as workload signals shown as help), §G; `docs/08` §6.
- **T6 (owner, Hebrew brief, translated).** "A company-wide task board with everything that happens, goals, who is handling what, all deliverables connected to the task, dependencies, schedules where relevant, what is stuck and on what. - **T7 (owner, 2026-10-28).** Grouping into higher-level groups, contexts and domains; things in more than one group (dependencies across domains: marketing waits for version X); drill into categories, then epics/projects, like zooming in and out of a map; a natural, coherent arrangement "more like a map", modified by the perspective of the viewer (CMO: marketing view; CTO: R&D view) and by the meeting's subject; tasks surfaced and prioritised per viewer. APIs into all the organisation's systems, replacing many. Lucid Total represents the company from the smallest detail to the highest goal and manages tasks, assignments, notifications and progress for everyone. Decisions implemented quickly, even big ones ("focus on Europe": compliance first, marketing budget, Europe attention for everyone, the rest maintained); strategy changes disseminated; who can do what managed in a flat organisation; anyone can suggest; prediction and transparency of change (small and large). The map: semi-physical space, dynamic surface, overlaps; inspirations from mapping and data presentation tools; coherent and easy; all functionality AI-driven; designed so AI tools and LLMs interact with it easily. → `docs/02` §2, §6; `docs/03`; `docs/08`.
- **T8 (owner, 2026-10-28, added context).** Lucid Total is a company operating system in the full sense: it runs the company, it does work, it manages the company; the goal is for the AI to fully manage the company and to facilitate decision making, taking more of the work over time. Design: Lucid Chat's look and feel, theming and a derived logo in the same visual language; apart from colours and fonts, the controls and look are those of a web-based management system for PC and mobile browsers. "Think deeply on how companies work." → `docs/00` §1, principles 9, 14, 15; `docs/02` §2 (functions, rhythms, flows, decision kinds, resources, feedback, the autonomy ladder); `docs/04-mocks/README.md` §Brand.

- **T17 (owner, 2026-10-28, later).** (1) All users can make suggestions and request features; things from support filter up as feature requests; any ask from another domain is surfaced to whoever is responsible to decide whether action is needed, what to do and whether it becomes a task. (2) The system must evolve with the company in function, reality, codebase and complexity: two people with direct focus today, more zoomed out and more people as it moves. - **T25 (owner, 2026-10-28, review of mocks v0.1).** The mocks and most of the interface "look like a classic project management system; this is not the feel I want". Instructions: (1) the phone maps are too condensed; the map must feel like a map, with zooming into areas that reveal sub-map divisions, and every region with a subject, a visual and a name. (2) More visual, breathing; products visible; the goal of a task, its definition of done, its products and what they look like must be clear at a glance; a decision needs context and visuals; a done item must show its implications. (3) Decision taking and the simulation of refocusing effort on X or Y must be clear. (4) The use cases must be visible in the mocks: daily use, meetings, a quick status; graphs and visualisations where appropriate; dashboard-like views per aspect of the company; dive-in from a quick sense of where things are. (5) Fable, like everything, is a configurable decision with understanding of its meaning. (6) Mechanisms for self-repair and self-improvement: the system is the core for its own internal operation, monitors itself, sees where it works well and where it breaks, and uses its own tools. (7) Core operations (overviews, decisions, updates) work in the system and integrate externally: work fully via Lucid Chat or email; agents live inside Slack or any messaging system, track progress, update tasks; automate everything the company does with the AI, leaving people to focus on tasks and helping them drive tasks. (8) Visualisation everywhere: data visualisation, images, outputs, mocks embedded. (9) Manage and orchestrate the AI processes; integrate strongly with Claude Code workflows to build new subsystems for itself and for the company's products. (10) An operating system with all the aspects an operating system has; the AI operating system of the future; the best possible context for every decision; easy research; creative, multi-faceted visualisation. (11) Infographics and an infographics view, made of components and mechanisms so the AI can create the right visual for a view, a ticket, a request, a dashboard, coding if needed; a growing set of options; oversight and validation of output; confirmation of results; follow-up, maintenance, monitoring; multiple levels of models by cost and specialisation. (12) Autonomy depends on domain, model and how well integrated the AI is; more decisions and actions managed by the system over time, as a built-in mechanism. (13) Complexity grows with the company while presentation stays clear and concise; an integrated work environment, not a place to close tickets. (14) AI-centric: the AI manipulates and shows the interface by chat or voice; existing interfaces remain to navigate. (15) Pleasant, visual, never overwhelming; the relevant context only; focus attention; minimal distractions and alarms; not too much data at once. → `docs/13-experience.md` (binding), `docs/14`, `docs/15`; `docs/00` principles 9 (rewritten), 19–22; `docs/01` §Q–§T; `docs/02` §5.0, §5.1, §5.9, §5.10, §5.12, §5.13, §6.3, §7.2, §7.10, §15, §16; `docs/03` revised; `docs/11` Phases 0–3; mocks v0.2 (v0.1 archived under `docs/04-mocks/v0.1/`).

- **T29 (owner, 2026-10-28, evening).** People do not create or arrange the interface; they use it: click when needed, type or speak instructions in context. Design effort therefore goes into how the AI composes, not into user assembly: the control library, the components, and the ability to add, modify and grow controls, layouts and visualisation mechanisms over time; contexts are many and varied, including images and, where relevant, generated images. Flexibility comes from the AI mechanisms; underneath, capabilities are collected, enhanced and grown. → `docs/00` principle 20 (amended), `docs/13` §8 (amended), `docs/02` §7.10, `docs/14` §9.

## 2026-10-28 · Lead decisions (pending the owner's confirmation)

- **T9 (lead).** Documentation order and set as in `docs/00` §7–§8; the methodology of Lucid Chat (walkthroughs, docs in the same change, lead orchestrates, Opus workers, Fable for core and security work, Railway-first, current majors, no GitHub Actions) is inherited verbatim.
- **T10 (lead).** Stack: TypeScript 6 / Node 22, Fastify 5, Drizzle + Postgres 18 on Railway, React 19 + Vite, SVG map with server-computed scenes, in-memory hybrid retrieval with a local embedding model, the Claude API through `packages/ai-gateway` (the official TypeScript SDK inside the adapter only), Railway Buckets. No Redis, NATS, vector DB or Kubernetes at the start (`docs/05` §2, §12).
- **T11 (lead).** Secrets travel as end-to-end encrypted envelopes sealed in the browser to recipients' keys; the server stores ciphertext only; the model never sees envelope contents (`docs/07` §5).
- **T12 (lead).** The autonomy ladder (observe · suggest · confirm · inform · run) is a setting per process; defaults for Lucid in `docs/02` §2.7; outbound channels off until enabled, each with a kill-switch.
- **T13 (lead).** The owner's inbox follows Lucid Chat's L425 rule: material decisions only, one at a time, full context and cost in plain words; small things are settings; very small ones the lead decides and logs here.
- **T14 (lead).** Phase order: Phase 0 the board and the coordinator protocol (the checklist replaced), Phase 1 the AI runs the operation, Phase 2 the map and decisions, Phase 3 company-wide, Phase 4 product (`docs/11`).
- **T15 (lead).** Workload signals about people (T5's "these employees aren't doing well") are implemented as explainable work signals shown to the person first and to the role holder as a workload concern, with thresholds as settings and no verdicts about people (UC-E4). Rationale: trust, labour-law exposure in several markets, and the owner's stated goal of helping the work move.
- **T16 (lead).** Model classes `fast` / `standard` / `strongest` / `fable` map to `claude-haiku-5-5` / `claude-sonnet-5-5` / `claude-opus-5-5` / `claude-fable-5-1` in configuration (2026-10 ids); the owner's chat defaults to `strongest`; Fable is enabled per company by the owner.

- **T18 (lead).** Asks are one kind (`ask`) with routing rules and a decision outcome, and proposals (T7) are asks that target a decision; one inbox, one ageing rule (`docs/02` §4.9).
- **T19 (lead).** Externals are **parties** with **engagements** and **compartments**, in three modes sharing one identity (direct, virtual, system); compartments are enforced at the data layer and by the retrieval and tool filters; engagement end dates revoke access automatically (`docs/02` §4.10, `docs/07` §3b).
- **T20 (lead).** Payroll-class data gets its own sensitivity class with server-side field-level encryption (not end-to-end, because cleared staff and payroll connectors must process it), read audit and an integrity-chained audit log; end-to-end envelopes remain the mechanism for secrets (`docs/07` §6b).
- **T21 (lead).** Company scale is a setting with four values whose change is proposed by the system and decided by the owner with a preview; Lucid runs at `founders` (`docs/02` §2.8).
- **T22 (lead).** Phasing: asks v0 and `founders` defaults in Phase 0; virtual parties in Phase 1; direct external views, compartments and the admission gate in Phase 2; payroll-class data, the board compartment, the scale model and the pen-test gate in Phase 3 (`docs/11`).
